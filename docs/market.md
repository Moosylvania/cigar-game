# Town Market

Open Market beside Research and Store to receive three customer orders. Each asks
for exact cigar varieties already unlocked by lifetime earnings. Standard, Select,
and Prestige orders pay 40%, 70%, and 100% over the normal sale value at generation.
Amounts scale with the largest Nursery's batch capacity, permanent batch research,
difficulty, and a gradual set multiplier capped at 1.5× after ten completed sets.

Quotes include permanent sale research, Rolling House level, and prestige bonuses.
Temporary Money Rush boosts do not inflate quotes. Prices and requirements are fixed
until the set is finished. Each subsequent set rotates customers and crop requests.

Deliver buttons contribute all matching whole cigars currently available toward an
order. Partial deliveries are saved and cannot also be exported or used on another
order. Completion pays once and counts toward lifetime earnings. All three orders
must finish before the next set automatically appears. There is no deadline.

The optional reservation checkbox prevents automatic exports from taking the
outstanding quantities needed across all open orders, including offline exports.
Surplus still sells. Reserved cigars occupy Depot storage until delivered; partial
deliveries let orders larger than the Depot's capacity be completed over time.
Reservations are off initially so opening the Market does not silently stop sales.

Old saves gain a board when first opened. Current orders, delivered counts, quoted
rewards, and reservations survive reload/export/import. Prestige clears the board
and partial deliveries along with the rest of the production economy.

Customer portraits share the game's motion clock, respect paused/reduced motion,
and unsubscribe when the panel closes. The dialog supports Escape, focus trapping,
focus restoration, and labeled progress bars.
